JOB TITLE: Data Analyst
COMPANY: GreenCart Retail (Bengaluru, on-site)

ABOUT THE ROLE
Help store and sales teams make decisions with data.

REQUIRED SKILLS
- SQL (complex queries, joins)
- Excel
- Power BI or Tableau dashboards
- Data cleaning
- Clear communication with non-technical teams

NICE TO HAVE
- Python and pandas
- Statistics
- Retail or sales experience

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Build and maintain weekly sales dashboards.
- Find trends and problems in sales data.
- Present findings to store managers.
